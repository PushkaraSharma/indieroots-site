About

A small studio. Two products.

IndieRoots builds focused apps for people who run things in India. Landlords. Gym owners. Operators who still live in spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads.

We keep products small on purpose. RentVelo is rent management. GymKarta is gym management. Each app does that job well instead of becoming a platform.

Privacy is part of the design. Where we can, data stays on the device. We do not sell operator data. We do not run a marketplace.

The studio is based in India. Reach us from the contact page, or open products to download RentVelo or GymKarta.
