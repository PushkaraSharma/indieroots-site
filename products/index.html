Rent management for Indian landlords. Collect rent, send receipts, and track balances from your phone.

  • Properties, tenants, and occupancy in one list
  • Monthly collection with paid, partial, and pending status
  • PDF rent receipts you can share on WhatsApp
  • Electricity readings and extras on the bill
  • Your data stays on your device, with optional Google Drive backup
